In today’s competitive job market, practical experience is invaluable. Regardless of your year of study, securing an internship is a crucial step toward building a successful career. This guide provides an overview of key local internship opportunities facilitated by Lingnan University.

## ITC STEM Internship Scheme
Designed specifically for students in STEM-related programmes, this scheme provides hands-on experience in innovation and technology fields such as coding, data analytics, and 3D modelling.

### Key Details
*   **Timeline:** Opens late April to early May.
*   **Mode:** Full-time.
*   **Period:** Typically 1 June – 31 August.
*   **Duration:** 28 to 59 consecutive days.
*   **Allowance:** Approximately HKD 393 per day (Verify current rates with OSA).

### Application Process
1.  **Verify Eligibility:** Check your programme’s [eligibility](Local_internship_eligibility.en.md).
2.  **Preparation:** Prepare your resume, personal details, a statement of purpose, and identify preferred roles.
3.  **Selection:** Browse the job list on the [official website](https://www.ln.edu.hk/osa/career-planning/internship-placements/local-internships/stem-internship-scheme) and select your top 3 preferences.
4.  **Submission:** Apply before the deadline.
5.  **Requirement:** If shortlisted, you **must** complete the [Pre-Internship Training Programme (PTP)](#pre-internship-training-programme-ptp) and meet any additional OSA requirements.
6.  **Non-local Students:** Meeting working [requirements](#non-local-students).

## Local Summer Internship Programme (LSIP)
The LSIP focuses on developing workplace adaptability and professional skills to improve post-graduation employability. It offers two streams: "Mainstream" and "Sports and Health."

### Key Details
*   **Timeline:** Applications typically open in late January to early February.
*   **Period:** June to August.
*   **Duration:** Approx. 8–12 weeks.
*   **Allowance:** Varies by company.
*   **Selection Criteria:** Candidates who are non-final year, highly motivated, and proficient in English, Putonghua, and Cantonese are preferred.

### Application Process
1.  **Preparation:** Prepare a professional resume and a photo in business attire.
2.  **Writing Requirements:**
    *   **English:** 150-word essay on your motivations and goals.
    *   **Chinese:** 150-word essay on your strengths and weaknesses.
3.  **Documentation:** Obtain your unofficial transcript (refer to this [guide](Local_internship_unofficial_transcript.en.md)).
4.  **Submission:** Submit the application via the online portal (University VPN required).
5.  **Interview:** Book your interview slot directly through the application form.
6.  **Post-Selection:** Shortlisted candidates must complete the [PTP](#pre-internship-training-programme-ptp). Selected students must complete a self-evaluation and attend a debriefing session.

## Government Post-Secondary Student Summer Internship Programme (GOVT)
Offered by the HKSAR Government, this programme provides summer opportunities for full-time undergraduate students who are permanent residents of Hong Kong.

### Key Details
*   **Timeline:** Irregularly throughout the year.
*   **Duration and Allowance:** Dependent on the specific department and role.
*   **Eligibility:** Full-time undergraduate students who are Hong Kong permanent residents.

### Application & Nomination
1.  **Monitor:** Regularly check the **[Job Information Platform (JIP)](https://jobs.ln.edu.hk/login)** using your student email.
2.  **Nomination:** The OSA Career Advising Services manages the nomination process. Selection is based on eligibility, skill matching, and specific departmental requirements.
3.  **Strategy:** Prepare a tailored CV, especially for specialized roles (e.g., data science or IT).

## Important Resources & Policies

### Pre-Internship Training Programme (PTP)

The PTP is **mandatory** for all OSA-administered internship and subsidy schemes. It covers vital topics like identifying employment traps and professional CV writing.

*   **Structure:** Includes compulsory/optional modules and LinkedIn Learning courses.
*   **Recognition:** Each workshop earns 1 ILP unit.
*   **Access:** Available via the [online platform](https://osa.ln.edu.hk/ptp). (University VPN required)

### Non-Local Students
To participate in local internships, non-local students must:

*   **NOL:** Obtain a **No Objection Letter (NOL)** from the Hong Kong Immigration Department alongside your student visa.
*   **Approvals:** Internships outside of the summer period require formal approval from your Department or School.
*   **Lead Time:** Processing can take several weeks; plan well in advance.

### Tips for Success Application

*   **Apply Early:** Programmes often utilize rolling admissions; applying as soon as the portal opens increases your chances.
*   **Professionalism:** Treat every internship application with the same rigor as a permanent job application.
*   **Financial Support:** If you secure an unpaid internship, contact the OSA to inquire about available subsidies.
