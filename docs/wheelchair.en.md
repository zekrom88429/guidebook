Lingnan University (Lingu) is a relatively wheelchair-friendly university. If the SEN (Special Educational Needs) team has not contacted you yet, please proactively reach out to them by sending an email to [sen@ln.edu.hk](mailto:sen@ln.edu.hk), calling 2616 7119 / 2616 7308, or filling out the online form at [https://www.ln.edu.hk/osa/sen](https://www.ln.edu.hk/osa/sen) (scroll down to the bottom of the page).

If you plan to live in student housing, Hall G and Hall H feature single wheelchair-accessible rooms. The restrooms inside are spacious and equipped with barrier-free facilities such as grab bars and shower stools, with all heights tailored for wheelchair users. Caregivers are also permitted to live in with you (the author was actually the first case). If you need this, let the SEN team know as soon as possible, and the Hostel Team will follow up.

Orientation and Exam Accommodations
Once your SEN registration is complete, staff will be assigned to assist you on New Student Orientation (NSO) day, so there is no need to worry. A major benefit of SEN services is special exam arrangements. As long as you submit the relevant reports and follow DSE-style accommodations, the university is generally very accommodating and lenient without making things difficult for you. Feel free to make requests without pressure. Exams typically do not take place in the large Chan Sui Kau Gymnasium, but rather in the adjacent table tennis or squash courts.

Activities and ILP PFW Requirements
The SEN team organizes various activities specifically for SEN students. If you worry about feeling pressured in other events, these can be great opportunities to make friends. Lingnan University does not exempt wheelchair users from the ILP PFW (Physical Education and Well-being) requirement (first-year entry students must earn at least 5 points). Since most activities are sports-oriented and hard for wheelchair users to participate in, lectures or SEN events offering ILP PFW points are in very high demand.

Campus Facilities and Environment
Lingnan's campus is relatively small; except for a few gardens and areas past the third pavilion, most places are accessible. However, due to ongoing construction of a new building, traveling from the Leung Kau Kui Building (LKK) toward the main entrance requires a detour near the canteen to find a ramp. It is recommended to plan extra travel time.

Campus Access and Restrooms

    Entrances: The main entrance has no barrier-free access—only stairs—and the stair lift is frequently out of order. Waiting for it takes longer than simply detouring to a side gate. It is recommended to enter via the footbridge or the North Gate (near Chelsea Heights or Fu Tai Estate).

    Restrooms: The most convenient restrooms are inside the residence halls, considering it takes a 10 to 15-minute wheelchair ride from PST or S.H. Ho Hall (SEK). If you need to use a restroom on campus, two larger options are recommended: the one at Learning Commons on the 2nd floor of Lau Chung Him Building (LYH), and the one outside the elevators on the ground floor of SEK. Other restrooms are usable, but tend to be cramped and less clean.

Transportation
Transportation options are basically the same as for everyone else. If you take bus K51 or K51A, getting off at the stop after "Lingnan University"—closer to Fu Tai Estate or the North Gate—makes it much easier to enter the campus, as the main entrance is impassable and the North Gate is far more convenient.

Social Life
Many wheelchair users worry about failing to make friends in university, but there is no need to overthink it at Lingnan. Everyone is very friendly, willing to greet you and hold doors open. Naturally, you still need to take the initiative to talk to people to build relationships. The author has experience joining a student society ("sheung chong"). Whether you choose to join a society or participate in O-day/O-camp depends entirely on your own physical condition. If interested, you can check with the societies beforehand or attend the Affiliated Societies Night on September 3 to look around without fear.
